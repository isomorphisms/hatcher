## Reading the list

- **Visually checked:** the supplied page image was inspected and the record's presence and location checked. This is not independent second-review evidence, a proof of the pictured mathematics, or permission to reuse artwork.
- **Text-located:** the text refers to the illustration, chart, or table, or preserves a recognizable displayed diagram. Appearance and boundaries have not been visually checked.
- **Candidate:** the passage suggests an image, but a separate supplied illustration still needs confirmation. Do not count candidates as confirmed figures.
- **Rejected:** visual review found no supplied illustration at the proposed location. Preserve the ID and correction history but exclude the record from active counts.

Pages in the inherited tables below are **printed pages**, not PDF viewer indices. A range is a search window, not a claim that a figure spans those pages. Exact PDF offsets are established only in the two visually checked inventories. Their source labels record observation dates, not frozen editions; no source-byte checksums or cross-copy byte identity have been established.

An unlisted page outside the completed ledgers has not been certified figure-free. Exercises asking the reader to draw something are not, by themselves, evidence of a supplied illustration. Inline graph pictures and algebraic diagrams are within scope, but ordinary formulas and isolated mathematical symbols are not counted as figures.

## Continuing the census

For every remaining source version, enumerate all PDF pages and keep printed labels separate from one-based PDF indices. Visually inspect even pages without searchable figure references. Confirm or reject candidates; keep existing IDs stable. Record exact locations, grouping decisions, repeated occurrences, source limitations, and corrections.

Obtain source-byte checksums before claiming immutable version pinning. Reconcile alternative editions and the EPS archive separately. A complete page sweep is not a unique-artwork count, a mathematical correctness proof, an independent acceptance receipt, or completion of another work's inventory.

This continuation added 33 records and rejected one old candidate: **60 + 33 − 1 = 92 active records**. It does not claim FP admission, dispatch, independent AICI qualification, merge, or release.
