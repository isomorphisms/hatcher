# Figure inventory

[Book catalog](../README.md)

**Initial inventory, not a complete census.** This list contains 60 entries: 45 located in the available text and 15 candidates requiring confirmation that a separate illustration is present. Three entries are tables. Some entries describe a group of panels, not one individual drawing. These numbers are therefore not a count of all figures in Hatcher's books.

The descriptions below are editorial descriptions, not copied captions. IDs such as `AT-001` are this project's identifiers, not figure numbers assigned by Hatcher. No PDFs, page images, or extracted illustrations are redistributed here.

## Reading the list

- **Text-located:** the available text refers to the illustration, chart, or table, or preserves a recognizable displayed diagram. Its appearance, boundaries, and panel count have not been visually checked.
- **Candidate:** an illustrated passage or its extracted layout suggests an image, but the presence of a separate illustration still needs checking. These entries must not be counted as confirmed figures.
- **Page:** the printed page number, not the PDF viewer's page index. A range is a search location, not a claim that a figure spans those pages. Exact placement remains to be checked against the stated version.

Visual inspection could not be completed in this pass. No row is marked visually verified. An unlisted page has not been certified figure-free. Exercises asking the reader to draw something are not, by themselves, evidence of an illustration supplied in the book.

That is the author's archive count, not the number inventoried below. The archive's individual filenames have not yet been enumerated or matched to book pages. The archive and the page inventory must eventually be reconciled; adding 158 to the row count would double-count overlapping material.

## Completing the census

For each source version, enumerate every PDF page, recording its printed label separately from its one-based PDF index. Visually review every page, including pages without searchable figure references. Confirm or reject candidates; split panel groups only when the source supports it. Keep editorial IDs stable and record corrections rather than silently renumbering existing entries.

Each confirmed record should eventually include the exact source version or checksum, printed page, PDF page, position on the page, kind, description, and verification evidence. Mark a work complete only after its entire page ledger has been checked, its diagrams and tables have been accounted for, and alternate editions or omitted source installments have been explicitly addressed.
