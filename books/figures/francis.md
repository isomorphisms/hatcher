# Francis — *A Topological Picturebook*: illustrated-figure inventory

[Back to Hatcher book catalog](../README.md)

**Author:** George K. Francis. **Work:** *A Topological Picturebook* (Springer, first published 1987; paperback reissue 2006, copyright 2007).

**Coverage:** **87/87 numbered figure groups** from the printed contents, plus **4/4 color plates**. These are chapter-figure groups, **not a verified count of individual panels or drawings**. Number and starting printed page are checked against the published table of contents. No full visual page-by-page census, panel transcription, or evaluation of the artwork has been performed.

**Evidence:** [Springer book record and chapter pagination](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-68120-7); [Ingram-supplied detailed printed contents transcribed by Booktopia](https://www.booktopia.com.au/a-topological-picturebook-george-k-francis/book/9780387345420.html); [Google Books bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/A_Topological_Picturebook.html?id=GtEPAQAAMAAJ). The book's original edition advertises **87 illustrations**, agreeing with the 5 + 18 + 9 + 8 + 8 + 13 + 10 + 16 count below.

**How to read:** `FR-05-07` is our stable editorial ID for chapter 5, numbered Figure 7. Francis also refers to individual panels by a row/column suffix: for instance `6:9(34)` means chapter 6, Figure 9, row 3, column 4. **Do not invent these panel coordinates:** they require page inspection. All subjects below are short editorial labels, **not quoted captions**. Figure number and starting printed page come from the book's contents; illustration **kind** is a provisional editorial classification, not publisher metadata. A title can refer to a multi-panel plate.

## Promising construction-driven examples

These are **implementation leads**, not evidence that the corresponding Francis figure has been reproduced or tested. Each illustration should be generated from an explicit mathematical construction, not animated by freehand deformation of a static image.

| Figure | Mathematical data to specify | Derived views and interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FR-01-01, FR-01-02 | Parametrized surface such as `z = x·y`, or equivalent ruling/slice families | Ruled lines, horizontal sections, normal field, perspective; varying the slice updates every view from the same equation. |
| FR-02-09–11 | A singular-map or immersion family and its projection | Display wireframe, silhouette/occluded lines, and resolved surface with a common geometry and the same parameter. |
| FR-03-01–05 | Camera center, viewing plane, projective maps, and cube coordinates | Multiple projections of the **same** cube; distinguish mathematically valid projective views from drawing tricks. |
| FR-05-03–05, FR-05-08 | Parametrized immersed surfaces, double-point loci, and singularity sets | Rotate camera independently of deformation parameter; verify which image crossings represent true self-intersections. |
| FR-06-08–09 | A continuous family of immersed spheres | Time-slider with surface immersion and embeddedness distinguished; inspect self-intersection sets independently of the projection. |
| FR-07-03–04, FR-07-07–09 | Braids / mapping classes and explicit generators | Strand motion, endpoints, surface curves and induced automorphisms remain synchronized. |
| FR-08-02–03, FR-08-11–12 | Polyhedral cells, face-pairing relations and attaching maps | Change between glued and exploded views while preserving incidence and orientation data. |
| FR-08-13–15 | Isotopies of a punctured-torus fiber and their induced action on homology | Move the fiber through a cycle; display the same transformation as a geometric motion and a 2×2 integer matrix. |

## Remaining verification

1. Visually inspect all printed pages and confirm that each of the 87 chapter-numbered headings has its corresponding artwork; split every multi-panel figure using Francis's row/column scheme without guessing.
2. Identify **unnumbered** figures, small in-text diagrams, variants, and any other artwork not included among the 87 numbered groups, recording page and the edition inspected.
3. Check printed 1987 and reissued 2006/2007 editions for changes in pictures and pagination. Avoid treating the total page count (which can include front matter) as printed figure pagination.
4. For each interactive candidate, specify the mathematical object, map, projection and invariants before specifying drawing styles or motion.

No figure scans or copies from the book are hosted here; use publisher or licensed reading access for the originals.
